Toasty HQ

Toasty HQ is a personal creator dashboard used only by the owner of the Toasty channels. It shows statistics and comments from the owner's own YouTube, Instagram and TikTok accounts in one place, and runs entirely on the owner's own computer.

Not a public service. There are no sign-ups and no other users. It only connects to accounts the owner controls, with the owner's permission.

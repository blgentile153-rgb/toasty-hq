Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 4, 2026

Toasty HQ is a personal dashboard used only by the owner of the Toasty creator accounts to view statistics and manage comments on their own YouTube, Instagram and TikTok content. It is not offered to the public and has no other users.

What data it accesses

Only when the owner links their own account, and only for that account:

  • YouTube: channel details, the owner's videos and their statistics and analytics, and comments on the owner's videos (to read, reply to, and moderate them).
  • Instagram: basic profile information, the owner's posts and their insights, and comments on the owner's posts (to read, reply to, and hide them).
  • TikTok: basic profile information (display name, username, avatar), follower and like counts, and the owner's public videos with their view, like, comment and share counts.

How the data is used and stored

  • Data is used only to show statistics and comments to the account owner.
  • All data, including access tokens, is stored locally on the owner's personal computer. It is not uploaded to any server, sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.
  • The app only takes an action (such as posting a reply or hiding a comment) when the owner clicks a button to do so. It never posts content or sends messages on its own.

Data deletion

To delete all data Toasty HQ holds:

  1. In Toasty HQ, open a platform's settings and click Unlink. This deletes the stored access token for that platform.
  2. Delete the creator-hub/data folder on the owner's computer to remove all stored statistics.
  3. Optionally, remove the app's access on each platform: Google account permissions, Instagram (Settings → Website permissions → Apps and websites), and TikTok (Settings → Security → Manage app permissions).

Because no data is stored anywhere other than the owner's own computer, these steps remove all of it.
